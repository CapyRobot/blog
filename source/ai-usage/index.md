**AI Usage Disclaimer**

I write every post myself. Once a draft is complete, I sometimes use AI as a reviewer, much like a human editor, to flag unclear passages, gaps in logic, or places where the writing could be tighter. I read each suggestion critically and decide whether to accept it, adapt it, or ignore it. I don't use AI to generate drafts, topics, or text for my posts.

I occasionally use AI as a research aid, to sanity-check a claim or answer a direct question. I treat its answers as a starting point, not a source.

The ideas, voice, and conclusions are mine, and so is responsibility for what's published.
